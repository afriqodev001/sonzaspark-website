actually build.

Not slides. Not simulations. Real circuits, real robots, real presentations — made by learners aged 8 to 18 in SonzaSpark classes, bootcamps and school clubs.

Electronics Ages 10–14

The $5 Arduino lamp

Students wire up a transistor, a resistor and an Arduino to control a 220V bulb. First brush with power electronics — and the moment the lamp lit, everyone cheered.

Simulation → Build Ages 8–16

Design before you solder

Learners prototype circuits in Tinkercad on real laptops, then build the same thing in hardware. Catching bugs on screen is cheaper than catching them on fingers.

Students build model structures and live demos for the public at Nane Nane and school exhibitions. Learning to explain the work is half the lesson.

What we teach

Six ways learners
become builders.

Every SonzaSpark learner moves between these areas as they grow — from making their first LED blink to shipping a project that solves a real-world problem.

Robotics

Motors, sensors, chassis, microcontrollers. Autonomous robots that avoid obstacles, follow lines, or deliver things across the room.

Coding

Scratch for the youngest, micro:bit block-code in the middle, Arduino C and Python for older students. Loops, logic, and small games.

3D Printing & Design

Design in Tinkercad, slice in Cura, print in PLA. Students make keychains, phone stands, replacement parts, and their own toys.

Electronics & IoT

LEDs, breadboards, Arduino, sensors, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi. Smart lights, motion alarms, and devices that talk to a phone.

Fabrication

Woodwork, cardboard engineering, laser engraving, and simple manufacturing. The physical half of every project.

Soft Skills

Pitching your project, working in a team, debugging under pressure, and explaining what you built to a parent, a peer, or a judge.

How it runs

Six ways SonzaSpark
reaches learners.

The program is designed to meet students wherever they already are — at home on weekends, at school during term, out in the community during school holidays, and online for everyone else.

01

Weekend Classes

Term-long cohorts, Saturdays or Sundays. Students move through beginner → intermediate → advanced robotics over three cycles.

02

Holiday Bootcamps

Intensive 1–6 week programs during school breaks. Robotics, 3D printing, IoT and innovation challenges.

03

School STEM Clubs

Weekly in-school clubs — Robotics, Coding, Girls in STEM, 3D Design, Smart Agriculture. SonzaSpark brings the curriculum, kits and facilitators.

04

Online Classes & Communities

Self-paced courses on our LMS, live workshops over video, and open communities on HyracHub — for learners anywhere in the world.

05

Mobile STEM Lab

Laptops, projectors, robotics kits and 3D printers packed into a travelling lab — reaching schools and communities that can't come to a center.

06

Teacher Training

We train teachers to run their own STEM clubs inside their schools — the fastest way for the program to scale beyond our direct trainers.

Also happening

Student Showcases

Every cohort ends with a demo day — students pitch and demo their builds to parents, teachers, and sometimes press.
